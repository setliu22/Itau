FINAL ARCHITECTURE SEARCH RESULTS
=================================

Dataset names used in the paper
-------------------------------

- Internal name `nocom` means **Woodbridge**.
- Internal name `new` or `mix65` means **Synthetic (Substitution)**.

Experimental protocol
---------------------

All names were rendered in DejaVu Sans as 32-pixel-high grayscale images with
a black background. Rendered images were cached before slicing. Architecture
and hyperparameter selection used the corresponding training and validation
splits only. The test splits were unavailable to the Optuna objective and were
used only after one winner per dataset had been frozen.

Each architecture had an independent Optuna study for each dataset. The fast
screening study used five trials, at most five epochs per trial, seed 7, TPE
sampling, and Hyperband pruning. Validation ROC-AUC was the selection metric.
One established baseline configuration was enqueued per study. TPE used two
startup trials and then proposed configurations from completed observations.
Hyperband used a minimum resource of two epochs, maximum resource of five
epochs, and reduction factor two. Completed and pruned trials counted toward
the five-trial screening budget; failed infrastructure trials did not.
The regular Transformer replacement study restricted the layer count to 1--2;
the superseded three-layer result was excluded from final selection.

After validation-only selection, the winning configuration for each dataset
was trained for exactly 25 epochs on the full training split. The checkpoint
with the highest validation ROC-AUC was evaluated once on the corresponding
test split. Threshold-dependent test metrics below use threshold 0.5 unless
explicitly labeled as using the validation-selected threshold.

Hyperparameter search spaces
----------------------------

Shared optimization parameters:

- Embedding dimension: 64, 128, 256
- Learning rate: 1e-4 to 2e-3, log scale
- Weight decay: 0, 1e-6, 1e-5, 1e-4
- Dropout: 0.0, 0.1, 0.2
- Batch size: 32, 64, 128; Interaction CNN used 32 or 64
- Remove renderer padding: false or true
- Early-stopping patience during screening: 2 or 3
- Seed: 7
- Screening budget: 5 Optuna trials and at most 5 epochs per trial

Sliced-model input parameters:

- Slice width: 6, 16, 32 pixels
- Stride: non-overlapping or 50% overlap
- Image height: fixed at 32 pixels
- Font/background: fixed DejaVu Sans, white glyphs on black background

Pair-scoring heads where applicable:

- Direct cosine similarity
- Cosine similarity plus linear calibration
- Symmetric [absolute difference, elementwise product] plus linear layer
- Symmetric [absolute difference, elementwise product] plus one-hidden-layer MLP
- MLP hidden ratio: 0.5, 1.0, 2.0 times embedding dimension
- MLP activation: ReLU or GELU

Architecture-specific parameters:

- Conv1D: 2 or 3 layers; kernel 3 or 5; ReLU or GELU; mean, max, or attention pooling.
- BiLSTM: 1 or 2 layers; hidden ratio 0.5 or 1.0; final hidden, mean, or attention reduction.
- Regular Transformer: 1 or 2 layers; 2, 4, or 8 heads; FFN ratio 1, 2, or 4; ReLU or GELU; mean or attention pooling; RoPE fixed.
- Cross-attention: separate fixed 1-block and 2-block families; 2, 4, or 8 heads; per-slice FFN ratio 1, 2, or 4; ReLU or GELU; mean or attention pooling.
- Interaction CNN: cosine or rich four-channel interaction map; 1--3 Conv2D layers; 16, 32, or 64 hidden channels; kernel 3 or 5; ReLU or GELU; masked mean, max, or mean+max pooling; linear or one-hidden-layer MLP classifier.
- Whole-image CNN baseline: no slicing or horizontal resizing; 2 or 3 stride-2 Conv2D layers; hidden channels 16, 32, or 64; embedding dimension 64, 128, or 256; kernel 3 or 5; ReLU or GELU; masked mean, max, or mean+max pooling; symmetric final head.

Best five-epoch validation screening results
--------------------------------------------

| Architecture | Woodbridge ROC-AUC | Synthetic (Substitution) ROC-AUC |
|---|---:|---:|
| Conv1D | 0.977430310 | 0.998745120 |
| Conv1D + BiLSTM | 0.994413778 | 0.999933760 |
| Conv1D + Transformer, 1--2 layers | 0.994442822 | 0.999428824 |
| Conv1D + 1-block cross-attention Transformer | 0.994962630 | 0.999682305 |
| Conv1D + 2-block cross-attention Transformer | 0.995493145 | **0.999960095** |
| Conv1D + Interaction CNN | **0.996273419** | 0.999827963 |
| Whole-image CNN, no slicing | 0.991435511 | 0.999873488 |

The sliced Conv1D baseline alone did not outperform the whole-image CNN. The
sequence-aware and pair-interaction models generally provided the improvement:
all sequence-aware models exceeded the whole-image CNN on Woodbridge, while
the two-block cross-attention model and BiLSTM slightly exceeded it on the
Synthetic (Substitution) validation split. The Synthetic (Substitution) scores
are close to saturation, so small differences should be interpreted cautiously.

Final architecture summary
--------------------------

| Dataset | Winning architecture | Input representation | Pair processor | Pooling and classifier | Parameters | Best epoch |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| Woodbridge | Conv1D + Interaction CNN | 32-pixel-high image; 16-pixel slices; stride 8 | Shared 2-layer Conv1D, 128-dimensional slice embeddings; rich four-channel all-pairs interaction map; 3-layer Conv2D with 32 channels and kernel 5 | Masked mean interaction pooling; 32-unit MLP | 301,793 | 10/25 |
| Synthetic (Substitution) | Conv1D + 2-block cross-attention Transformer | 32-pixel-high image; 6-pixel non-overlapping slices | Shared 2-layer Conv1D, 128-dimensional slice embeddings; two bidirectional 4-head cross-attention-plus-FFN blocks with residual connections | Attention pooling; symmetric linear head over absolute difference and elementwise product | 521,987 | 19/25 |

Both winners use shared Conv1D weights for the two names. The Woodbridge model
constructs one joint pairwise interaction representation, whereas the Synthetic
(Substitution) model maintains two directionally cross-attended sequences before
pooling and symmetric comparison.

Important reporting constraints
-------------------------------

- The architecture comparison table is validation-only.
- Only the frozen winner for each dataset was evaluated on test.
- Do not report test metrics for non-winning architectures.
- Do not include the superseded three-layer regular Transformer result.
- Five Optuna trials per architecture is a screening study, not an exhaustive search.
- Whole-image CNN configuration files contain unused inherited Conv1D metadata fields; the actual model contains only Conv2D layers and uses the `image_cnn_*` fields.
