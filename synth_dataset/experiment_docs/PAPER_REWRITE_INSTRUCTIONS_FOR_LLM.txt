Remove obsolete model-selection material
----------------------------------------

Remove sections, paragraphs, tables, and captions describing the old manual or

Replace that material with the current validation-only Optuna protocol from
FINAL_ARCHITECTURE_SEARCH_RESULTS.txt. Clearly state that:

- Hyperparameters were optimized independently for Woodbridge and Synthetic (Substitution).
- Each architecture used an independent Optuna study.
- The screening budget was five trials with at most five epochs per trial.
- TPE sampling, Hyperband pruning, and seed 7 were used.
- Validation ROC-AUC was the selection metric.
- Test data was not used during architecture or hyperparameter selection.
- The regular Transformer search was restricted to one or two layers; the superseded three-layer result must not appear.
- After selection, the winner was trained for 25 epochs and the highest-validation-ROC-AUC checkpoint was evaluated on test.

Add a short Methods subsection titled **Validation-only hyperparameter
optimization** (or an equivalent title). Explain the Optuna logic rather than
only naming the library:

- Each architecture and dataset had an independent study with a five-trial budget.
- Every trial jointly sampled all applicable input, optimization, pooling, and architecture-specific parameters.
- Each trial trained for at most five epochs on the full training split and returned validation ROC-AUC.
- One established baseline configuration was enqueued in each study.
- The TPE sampler used seed 7 and two startup trials; after startup it proposed configurations using the completed observations.
- Hyperband used minimum resource 2 epochs, maximum resource 5 epochs, and reduction factor 2, allowing weak trials to be pruned.
- Completed and pruned trials counted toward the five-trial screening budget; failed infrastructure trials did not.
- No test score, thresholded test metric, or test example influenced Optuna or final architecture selection.
- Describe this explicitly as a compact screening study, not an exhaustive hyperparameter search.

Required interpretation
-----------------------

Explain the whole-image CNN as an unsliced Siamese Conv2D baseline operating on
native-width cached DejaVu Sans images. State the result accurately:

- Slicing alone did not provide the gain, because the sliced Conv1D baseline was weaker than the whole-image CNN.
- On Woodbridge, BiLSTM, regular Transformer, both cross-attention models, and Interaction CNN exceeded the whole-image CNN; the plain sliced Conv1D baseline did not.
- On Synthetic (Substitution), the two-block cross-attention model and BiLSTM slightly exceeded the whole-image CNN, but validation ROC-AUC values were near saturation and small differences should be interpreted cautiously.
