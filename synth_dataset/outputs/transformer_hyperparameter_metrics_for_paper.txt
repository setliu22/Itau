Suggested paper note
--------------------
Future experiments should perform model-specific hyperparameter tuning for each
architecture, since the transformer results are sensitive to slicing and pooling:
the 32/16/mean configuration achieved validation ROC-AUC 0.8538, while the
16/16/attention configuration achieved validation ROC-AUC 0.9963.
